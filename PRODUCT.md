# Robison Ancestry — product context

register: brand (landing/overview) wrapped around product (data views)

## What it is
A static genealogy site generated from YAML evidence files: nine generations of the
Robison family from County Tyrone (Ulster) → Bay of Quinte, Ontario → Carman, Manitoba →
the BC interior. Every fact cites a source; conflicting claims coexist; parent links carry
confidence levels. The site is the public reading surface for that model.

## Users
- Barrie (maintainer) and extended family — cousins, siblings — most of them non-technical,
  reading on laptops and phones, often arriving from a link in a family email.
- Occasional serious genealogists cross-checking sources.

## Voice
Archival, sturdy, honest. The register of a bound county history or a parish register,
not a SaaS product. Three brand-voice words: **ledger-bound, weathered, exact**.

## Identity
The family claims a Clan Gunn sept connection (Robison/Robson are recognized septs).
The palette is drawn from the modern Clan Gunn tartan: deep pine green, dark navy,
black, with the thin red overcheck as the accent. The sept claim itself is an *open
research question* — the site says so honestly; the palette is heritage, not proof.

## Anti-references
- Ancestry.com's leaf-green corporate chrome.
- Generic SaaS landing pages (hero metric cards, gradient text).
- Tartan cosplay: no plaid backgrounds, no clip-art crests, no Celtic knot borders.

## Strategic principles
- The evidence model is the product. Confidence levels and source citations must stay
  legible everywhere.
- Print-friendly: relatives will print the chart.
- Everything is generated; design lives in famtree/site_template.html and famtree/render.py.
