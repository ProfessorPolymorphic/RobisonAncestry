Nine generations, one paper trail.

The Robison line runs from Presbyterian County Tyrone through the Bay of Quinte shore of Upper Canada, the wheat towns of Manitoba, and the rail country of the British Columbia interior. This site is the family's evidence file: every fact cites a source, disagreements between sources are kept in the open, and every parent–child link says how well it is established.

    Where things stand

    The Clan Gunn question

    An open question, honestly held Robison is a recognized sept of Clan Gunn, and Paul A. Robison's family history asserts the connection. The documentary trail, though, still begins with Hugh in Tyrone. This site wears the tartan's colours; the proof is a research item, not a settled fact.

    About this site

    This page is generated from four YAML files, covering people, families, sources and places, kept in the repository. Every fact cites a source. Where sources disagree, both claims are kept; the first listed is shown on the chart and the rest appear under Conflicts. Each parent→child link carries a confidence level, explained on the overview page.

    Contributing

    Edit the YAML in the repository (the web editor works fine). When a document contradicts what is already recorded, add a second event with its source rather than overwriting. The site rebuilds automatically on every change. Full conventions are in the repository README.
